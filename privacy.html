Effective November 17, 2026

This policy explains what data the Porog app (“Porog”, “we”) handles and why. In short: your trips stay on your device and in your own iCloud. We do not run servers that store your trips, and we cannot see them. The app shows ads and uses analytics through the third-party services listed below.

Data that stays with you

  • Trips, countries, settings and attached proof files are stored on your device. iCloud sync is on by default and can be turned off in Settings; while it is on, a copy is kept in your private iCloud storage, and daily backups in your private iCloud database. Apple stores this data; we have no access to it.
  • No account. Porog has no sign-in and does not ask for your name or e-mail.
  • Photos and Calendar. If you turn on trip suggestions and allow access, Porog reads only the date and location of photos and calendar events, on the device, to suggest the start or end of a trip. Photos and events never leave your device.
  • Camera. If you take a picture of a passport stamp or a ticket, or scan a boarding pass, the camera is used only at that moment. The boarding pass is read on the device; a picture you keep is stored with the trip like any other proof file.
  • Location. If you turn on trips from location (off by default) and allow access, Porog checks which country you are in. With background access it uses the significant-change service and visit monitoring (cell towers and Wi-Fi). The coordinate is turned into a country on the device with bundled country outlines, without any network request, and then discarded. Only the country and the date are kept, on this device only: they are not synced to iCloud and not sent to anyone.
  • Assistant. On devices with Apple Intelligence, the assistant answers with Apple’s on-device language model. Your questions, the answers and the trips they refer to stay on the device.
  • Apple Watch and Apple TV. The Watch app receives your counts from your iPhone directly. The Apple TV app reads the copy of your trips in your own iCloud.
  • Sharing you start. When you send a card in Messages, share a report, a video or a wallpaper, or watch the flight globe together over SharePlay, the people you share with see what that card, file or replay shows (for SharePlay: the countries, dates and lengths of the replayed stays). Porog never shares anything on its own.
  • App Clip. The 90/180 calculator App Clip keeps the stays you enter on the device only.
  • Notifications, widgets, Shortcuts and Spotlight work on the device only.

Tracking and consent

Porog asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before ads may use your advertising identifier. If you decline, ads are not personalised with it, and analytics data is not used for advertising. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Porog also asks for consent through Google’s consent form before ads and analytics store data; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The one-time Porog Pro purchase turns off all ads.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Legal bases (EEA, UK)

Ads and analytics rely on your consent. Crash and performance diagnostics rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. Data that stays on your device or in your iCloud is processed to provide the app’s features you ask for.

Retention and deletion

  • Settings → Delete iCloud data erases the copies of your data and the backups in your iCloud and turns sync off.
  • Deleting the app removes all data stored on the device, including the location history.
  • Data held by third-party services is kept according to their policies. You can ask us to help with a deletion request at the address below.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Most data is under your control in the app and in iCloud; for anything else, contact us.

Children

Porog is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
