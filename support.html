How do I restore Porog Pro?

Open Settings in Porog and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

My trips do not sync between devices

Make sure both devices use the same Apple Account in iCloud, and iCloud sync is on in Porog Settings.

How do I delete my data?

In Porog Settings tap “Delete iCloud data”: this erases the copies of your data and the backups in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on the device.

Is Porog legal or tax advice?

No. Porog is a counting tool. Rules change and depend on your situation, so confirm important decisions with the authorities or a professional.
